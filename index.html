QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before you
take the next step.

Can you guarantee I will get my money back?

No. Recovery depends on facts, available routes, third parties, and jurisdiction. A review cannot guarantee recovery or a particular result.

Is Orvanta a law firm?

The information on this website is not legal advice or representation. Legal representation requires a separate engagement with a suitably licensed local lawyer.

What should I prepare?

A timeline, platform names and URLs, payment confirmations, relevant messages, account statements, and any complaint references. Keep originals and redact unrelated sensitive information from working copies.

Should I share passwords or connect my wallet?

No. Never share passwords, seed phrases, private keys, verification codes, or banking credentials. This site does not accept document uploads or wallet connections.

What if a transaction was unauthorised?

Contact your bank or payment provider directly through a verified channel to discuss security measures and dispute options. Ask about applicable deadlines. This website cannot freeze transactions or initiate disputes.

Can I submit a request or buy a service here?

Online submission and payments are currently unavailable. Any future service scope, pricing, and engagement terms require written confirmation.

Will I need a lawyer in my country?

Legal advice and representation depend on local rules and your situation. If needed, engage a suitably licensed lawyer under a separate agreement. Referral and representation are not guaranteed.
